Travel info and what to bring.

Choosing Wild Wave Crew means choosing safety, experienced guides and a day you will never forget. Paddle with us through the Nephi, Mosiah and Lamanite Rivers, where you can go from peaceful into huge adrenalin in seconds and enjoy nature at its most beautiful. Get ready!

What you'll have included for your trip?

  • Professional guide, instruction, and equipment.
  • Wetsuits and splash jackets if required.
  • Delicious fresh meals.
  • River shuttle to and from the Meet Place for your trip.
  • We have some valuable insights into dressing for each season. Our team will guide you on the essentials that will keep you comfortable during your experience.

What time should I have to be there?

Please arrive one hour before your rafting time so our guides can give you a brief safety instruction and get everyone ready for the river.
